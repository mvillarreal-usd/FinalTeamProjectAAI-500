# FinalTeamProjectAAI-500
Final Team Project for AAI-500 

The dataset represents ten years of medical information on diabetes and all the variables that healthcare professionals review when dealing with this disease. This dataset has over 100,000 inputs and 47 features, which we will be able to analyze and clean based on our needs.

URL for the selected dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/296/diabetes+130-us+hospitals+for+years+1999-2008

The dataset we chose was Diabetes 130-US Hospitals for Years 1999-2008 (Clore et al., 2014),  and our objective was to identify which variables were strongly associated with hospital readmissions. By analyzing the data, we aimed to determine which factors were more strongly associated with a patient being readmitted to the hospital. 
The dataset contains ten years of medical information from patients with diabetes and includes variables related to their hospital encounters. This dataset has over 100,000 encounters and 47 features, which we examined and cleaned based on our research objectives. 
To investigate these relationships, we performed data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and machine learning analysis. Data cleaning helped prepare the dataset for EDA by addressing missing values and variables with little variation. We then used EDA to examine categorical variables through frequency and relative frequency distributions to better understand the characteristics of patients and patterns in hospital readmissions.
For our model analysis, we chose to use a Bayesian Network, which was developed using the PC algorithm for structure learning and Bayesian estimation for calculating conditional probabilities. We used this model to examine the relationship between patient characteristics and hospital readmissions. This helped us examine statistical dependencies between variables and estimate the probability of hospital readmission based on previous patient information.
